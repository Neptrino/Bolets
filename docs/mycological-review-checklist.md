## Suggested scope if review is commissioned later

1. Existing high-risk content: `/bolets-verinosos`, `/bolets-comestibles`, deadly/toxic species pages and the identification comparisons. Check that culinary labels, processing conditions and lookalike differences never imply a safe identification from one trait.
2. `/fals-rossinyol`: verify the distinction between *Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca*, *Cantharellus cibarius* and *Omphalotus olearius*. Review the limits of the comparison, variability and the wording around differing consumption classifications.
3. `/bolets-de-soca`: verify the breadth and regional use of the popular name. Do not collapse it into *Kuehneromyces mutabilis* or another single species. Check the wood-fungi examples and the observation guidance.
4. Supplementary gallery media: verify species identity against original source images, licensing/provenance and whether captions could mislead. The 66 new alt descriptions describe visible composition only; `identificationReference: false` is unchanged. Two existing Ramaria gallery assets appear to show different crops of the same specimen/photo; do not present them as independent corroboration.
