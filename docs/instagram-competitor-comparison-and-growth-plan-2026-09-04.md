# Instagram competitor comparison and growth plan — 4 September 2026

## Verdict

`@bolets.app` has 12 followers after two weeks of automated publishing. The two Catalan accounts that lead this niche do not win with more data or better cards; they win with real forest footage, named humans, Reels, community co-authored posts and debate hooks. Our automated text cards reach 5–12 accounts each, have produced 0 comments, 0 saves and 0 follows in 30 days, and the daily feed card is being deleted after publication anyway. The one post that reached 106 accounts was a real mushroom photo with a human caption and three hashtags.

Only one other account sells a similar promise on Instagram: `@boletada`, the account of the paid predictor boletada.cat, opened on 28 August and at 209 followers after seven posts. Its map screenshots get 1–7 likes, the same floor as ours; the one post that worked for it (54 likes) was a weekend-planning hook. A daily, zone-by-zone conditions map is still our defensible angle, but it should become a weekly narrated Reel plus a daily Story, not a daily static feed card, and it should ship before the competitor owns the format. The website competitors (Va de Bolets, Fotos de Bolets, bolets.com) have no meaningful Instagram presence, so Instagram is an open flank if we play the community game rather than the automation game.

Recommended target: 150 followers by 2 October, 500 by 30 October, 1,000 by 30 November (season end). These are judgment calls; there is no growth history to extrapolate from.

### @boletada — the direct product competitor

`@boletada` is the account of boletada.cat, a Catalan-only subscription service (€19.99 a year as a "pioneer" price for the first 100 subscribers, then €29.99 a year) that sells a daily-recalculated map of favourable zones by species, a species guide and a best-practices page, with native apps promised. The map offers nine selectable species (rovelló/pinetell, cep, llenega negra, trompeta de la mort, rossinyol, camagroc, múrgola, ou de reig, fredolic) at comarca level with a detailed/station toggle on an Esri basemap; the guide lists 23 species with no author, photo credits or sources. We publish 62 species profiles with cited sources and 86 zone pages. The site names no team or company and publishes no social links. It is already listed for SE Ranking tracking in `docs/seo-launch-operations.md`. The account opened on 28 August 2026 and had 209 followers, 43 following and seven posts on 4 September.

What this tells us:

- It posts the same kind of content we do (map screenshots, text overlays, product captions) and gets the same engagement floor (1–7 likes, no comments). Static map cards do not engage in this niche whoever publishes them.
- The single outlier is the weekend-planning hook, which is the exact hook of the Thursday forecast Reel in this plan. Ship ours first and make it better: real footage, a human voice, named zones and species, and a dated evidence trail.
- 209 followers on 77 total likes in seven days is not explained by the grid. The likely sources are seeding through personal networks and Facebook groups and possibly paid boosts (one Reel at 54 likes against 1–7 elsewhere fits a boosted post). This cannot be confirmed logged-out. The lesson is that at this stage followers come from seeding and distribution, not from posting; Phase 0 now includes an explicit seeding push.
- Positioning: they charge for a map; we are free, cover 62 species, publish zone pages and dated readings, and offer a private field notebook. Whether to say "gratuït" out loud is a product decision, but the profile must make the free daily map and the species guide unmistakable.
- Do not propose Collab posts to a direct product competitor. Monitor it monthly through the benchmark CSV.

## What the leaders do that we do not

1. **Real footage and real people.** Every high-engagement post sampled shows a mushroom in a forest, a basket, or a person. Ours show text on a coloured background; Instagram's own alt text describes them as "image of text".
2. **Reels first.** Both leaders are effectively Reels-only in season. We have published Reels once, in a cluster.
3. **Co-authored posts.** The largest account is built on Instagram Collab posts; even a 938-follower Spanish account uses them to borrow reach. We have none.
4. **Conversation hooks.** A single question ("Tallar o arrancar?") produced 213 comments. We ask nothing and have 0 comments.
5. **Community intake.** "Envia les teves fotografies", "Etiqueta'ns", "¡Etiquétanos!" appear in three bios. We have a share page that pushes our cards out, but no way for people to send anything in.
6. **Season memory.** Highlights named "Temp 2022 … Temp 2026" and species-named highlights make a profile scannable. We have no highlights.
7. **Spacing and consistency.** Leaders post one or two items a day, spread out. We post nothing for days and then five items in three minutes.

What we have that none of them do: a daily, dated, zone-level conditions map; species-by-habitat pages for 62 species; a Remotion pipeline that can animate the map (the detailed-map Reel composition and the "avui evolution" capture already exist in `video/`); and a website with organic search traffic that can push visitors to the account.

## Growth plan

### Phase 0 — hygiene (this week, about two hours)

1. Retire the daily feed card. Keep the 07:00 Story (it is the habit format and does not clutter the grid). If the deletions were not intentional, find out who or what is deleting posts before touching the job.
2. Add a persisted publication ledger keyed by (channel, kind, Catalonia date) in Supabase so a job cannot publish the same item twice, and route all test publications to a Buffer draft or a private test channel. The Buffer date-marker lookup is not sufficient on its own, as 1 September showed.
3. Space feed posts: at most two per day, never less than three hours apart. Launch sets like the 4 September promo go out over a week.
4. Rewrite the profile. Name field: `Bolets Atles · Predicció de bolets Catalunya`. Bio (under 150 characters, all Catalan):

   > Condicions per trobar bolets a Catalunya, cada dia 🍄
   > Mapa per zones i espècies · Guia de 62 bolets
   > Mai revelem llocs ↓ Mapa d'avui

5. Create four highlights: **Avui** (archive of the daily Story), **Com llegir el mapa**, **Espècies**, **Seguretat**.
6. Follow 100–150 relevant accounts (creators, associations, natural parks, fairs, local tourism offices, the accounts listed above) and spend ten minutes a day leaving substantive comments. A 24-following account with no comments looks automated because it is.
7. Seed the account once the profile is fixed: tell personal networks and WhatsApp groups, post one genuinely useful map card with the handle in the large Catalan boletaire Facebook groups, and notify existing bolets.app users where a channel exists (notebook users, any mailing list). Optional: a €50–100 boost on the best-performing Trial Reel to measure follows per euro before deciding whether paid reach is worth anything.

### Phase 1 — content reset (September)

Weekly calendar (Europe/Madrid), four feed posts and seven Stories per week:

| When | Format | Purpose | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily 07:00 | Story: today's map | Habit; archive into the Avui highlight | Automated |
| Tue, Thu, Sat morning | Add a poll or quiz sticker to the Story ("Sortiu aquest cap de setmana?", "Quin bolet és?") | Replies and Story reach | Human, 5 min |
| Mon 18:30 | **Species Reel**, 15–25 s, vertical, real photos: three identification cues, one confusion species, habitat and season, never a place | Saves; replaces the species carousel, which reaches 5–9 accounts | Automated render + human check |
| Wed 19:00 | **Education carousel** (keep), but slide 1 is a real photo with a question, not a text card | Saves, shares | Automated |
| Thu 19:00 | **"Previsió boletaire del cap de setmana" Reel**, 20–40 s: animated map over the last 7 days, which zones rose or fell and why (rain window, temperature), which species are in window; human voice-over; CTA "Desa-ho per dissabte" and "Envia-ho al teu company de sortida" | The unique format; saves and sends are the two strongest ranking signals | Automated map + human voice |
| Fri 12:00 | Story reminder of the forecast Reel with a poll | Second exposure | Human, 2 min |
| Sun 18:00 | **Community post**, rotating: "Mapa vs realitat" (a submitted find, comarca only, next to what the map said that day) or an evidence-based debate ("Tallar o arrancar: què diuen els estudis?", "Quants dies després de la pluja surten els rovellons?", "Cistell o bossa?") | Comments, Collab reach | Human |

Rules for every post:

- Real photo or footage in the first frame. Text cards only as later slides.
- First caption line carries the keywords people search ("Predicció de bolets Catalunya · setmana 37"); hashtags limited to three to five niche tags (`#bolets #boletaires #rovellons #ceps #boletsdecatalunya`).
- Catalan first. Reels get a one-line Spanish summary in the caption so Spanish-speaking boletaires (a large share of the ecosystem, see @bolets_kanyas and @boletusaddicted) can find them.
- Test new hooks as Trial Reels before committing a format.
- Every Reel ends with one ask only: save, send, or answer.

Real imagery is the bottleneck. Options, in order: our own outings; licensed gallery photos already in `data/species-gallery-media.ts` where the licence permits social reuse (check each); community submissions with written consent.

### Phase 2 — distribution (October–November, peak season)

1. **Collab posts, one per week.** Ask creators to co-author: they supply the forest Reel, we supply the map overlay for that comarca and date. Candidates from this research: @reani86, @bolets_kanyas, @boletusaddicted, @cepsibolets (feeds a Facebook group), @bolets_de_l_emporda, @bolets.cat, local tourism accounts that already post about bolets (Turisme Cerdanya), natural parks (Montseny, Cadí-Moixeró), mushroom fairs (Castellterçol, Setcases, Llagostera, Seva) and mycological societies. Collab invitations must be sent from the Instagram app; Buffer cannot do it.
2. **Pitch @catalunya_bolets a weekly forecast Collab.** Their model is co-authored posts from the community; a weekly map Reel is content they cannot make themselves, and one accepted Collab exposes us to 27,000 followers. Use the playbook's outreach message with this concrete proposal.
3. **Site to Instagram funnel.** Embed the latest forecast Reel on `/bolets-avui`; add a follow prompt on species pages and in the field notebook; in the share-card flow suggest tagging `@bolets.app` when a user posts a card to their Story, then reshare it.
4. **Facebook groups and WhatsApp.** The forecast Reel is the most shareable thing we make. Post it natively (as a person, not a page) in the large Catalan boletaire Facebook groups each Thursday and add "Envia-ho al grup" to the Reel caption.
5. **Community intake.** Build "Envia la teva troballa": photo, comarca, date, consent, no coordinates. Approved submissions become "Mapa vs realitat" posts with the map score for that comarca and date. This turns the niche's dominant content (harvest photos) into our format.
6. **Giveaway once, at about 500 followers.** A field guide with a mycological society partner, follow-and-tag mechanic. Small accounts in the niche have used this; it works once, not repeatedly.

### Phase 3 — off-season (December–February)

Reduce to two feed posts a week: identification and safety education, retrospectives ("Què va passar al mapa aquesta temporada?"), recipes when the recipe section ships, and the winter species (camagrocs, trompetes, llenegues) that keep interest alive.

Review weekly in Instagram Insights (follows, non-follower reach, sends, saves, average watch time) and in `/admin/publicacio`. Keep the playbook's four-week kill-or-keep rule for each format.

### Effort

The current playbook budgets 15 human minutes a week. The evidence says that is not enough in season: automated cards reach single digits, and everything that reaches people in this niche involves a person. Budget two to three hours a week from September to November: one voice-over, one outing or photo session, daily replies and comments, and one collab conversation.

## What not to do

- No follow/unfollow churn, no bought followers, no engagement pods.
- No reposting other accounts' content outside Collab posts; ten reposts in 30 days removes the account from recommendations.
- No daily near-identical feed images; no clusters of posts.
- No collection locations, ever, including in submitted photos and Story replies.
- No `#viral #parati` hashtag padding; the leader does it and its own Reels still get under 100 likes.
