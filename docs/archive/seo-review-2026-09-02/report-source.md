# Bolets.app SEO review — canonical findings

Snapshot: 2026-09-02. Scope: Spain and Catalonia, with Catalan and Spanish search demand.

## Executive finding

Bolets.app has strong crawl hygiene (SE Ranking audit 95/100, zero critical errors) and early organic traction, but growth is constrained by indexation depth, weak click-through on high-impression hubs, limited practical-content breadth, and no measurable authority in SE Ranking.

## First-party performance

- Google Search Console: 195 clicks, 7,140 impressions, 2.7% CTR and average position 27.7.
- Selected range was three months, but available chart data covered 2026-08-11 through 2026-08-31.
- Index coverage: 158 indexed, 52 not indexed, 210 known. Forty pages were discovered but not indexed.
- Biggest CTR opportunities: `/bolets` (1,111 impressions, 7 clicks) and `/bolets-d-estiu` (643 impressions, 8 clicks).
- Best-performing page: `/zones/ceps` (47 clicks, 611 impressions).

## Tracked search visibility

- 145 tracked terms across Google Spain Catalan, Google Spain Spanish and Google Mobile Catalonia/Catalan.
- Current project visibility: 0.54% overall; 66 tracked engine-keyword combinations in the top 10.
- Strong/near-win terms include `parts d’un bolet`, `bolets verinosos de Catalunya`, `mapa bolets Catalunya`, `ceps bolets`, `bolet rossinyol`, `pinetells`, `bolets de primavera` and `cama de perdiu confusions`.

## Content gap conclusion

Priority gaps are bilingual Catalan–Spanish–scientific names, a practical responsible hunting guide, recipes by species, species-specific preservation, and confusion/poisoning-response coverage. Secondary gaps are tree/forest habitat, weather interpretation and mycology fundamentals. Cultivation is a weak product fit and should be deferred.

## Method and limitations

- Search volume, SEO difficulty, CPC and paid competition are SE Ranking estimates for Spain. Paid competition is not organic difficulty.
- The SE Ranking bulk raw competitor-keyword export endpoint was unavailable; analysis used accessible tracked-project, competitor and related-keyword datasets.
- Google Search Console's external-links report was still processing.
- Mushroom demand is seasonal. Volumes should be refreshed before major autumn publishing decisions.
- Editorial work must not reveal sensitive ecological spots, fabricate numerical ecology, imply expert review, or treat image identification as proof of edibility.

## Source ledger

- Google Search Console: first-party queries, pages, index coverage and sitemap status.
- SE Ranking: tracked rankings, visibility, competitor benchmark, keyword metrics and technical audit.
- Bolets.com: practical hunting, rain-data and recipe depth.
- La Casa de las Setas: tree/habitat and mycology-fundamentals depth.
- Bolets.app repository sitemap: current owned-content inventory.
- Generalitat de Catalunya mushroom guidance: safety/authority reference.
