# Species icons — regenerated-set recheck, 20 September 2026

Dated follow-up to the [numbered-icon review](species-icon-numbered-review-2026-09-20.md). This records the updated artwork, not a scientific or independent mycological sign-off.

## Coverage

The folder now has **62 PNGs representing all 62 prompted species**. Chroogomphus rutilus and Marasmius oreades have been added. One new Tricholoma terreum file replaces the former v1/v2 pair. Eleven other existing filenames have changed hashes. All 14 new or changed files were visually reviewed; the other 48 match the previous hashes and retain their prior screening status.

## Result

Most requested improvements are visible. **One correction remains: 45, Lepiota brunneoincarnata, needs visibly free gills.** The reduced ring is good, but the blades still appear to meet the stem instead of ending short of it. A narrow visible annular separation should be shown on both specimens. [Live profile morphology](https://bolets.app/bolets/palometa-metzinosa), [European mycological description](https://ambbresadola.it/funghi_tossici/lepiota-brunneoincarnata/).

**21, Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca, retains a smaller refinement:** make the blade edges thinner and less rounded. Repeated forking is now clear, but some thick highlights still suggest fleshy ridges. [UBC description and reference imagery](https://explore.beatymuseum.ubc.ca/mushroomsup/H_aurantiaca.html).

The gill junctions of 11 and 40 are improved but graphically scalloped. They are adequate as simplified icon cues for this screening; do not use them as literal anatomy diagrams without a clearer side view of the notches. The pale flecks on 24 are somewhat decorative, but its felted surface and lower adult peak address the original refinement. No further regeneration is prioritized for those three on this pass.
