# Species icon review — 20 September 2026

Dated visual screening of all 62 raw PNGs in `assets/illustrations/generated/species-icons/`. This is a separate review from the illustrated field cards. All icons were inspected on labelled 390-pixel contact sheets; the eight priority cases were also inspected individually at 800 pixels. No images were modified. Prompts were not used as evidence of anatomical correctness.

## Conclusion

Eight icons merit correction because an important feature is misleading or insufficiently distinguished. Nine more would benefit from refinement. The other 45 show no conspicuous contradiction at this screening scale; this is not a scientific sign-off or independent mycological review. Simplification, natural variation and an unscaled drawing limit what can be concluded. The findings are about representative educational depictions, not whether every drawn feature is biologically impossible.

## Secondary refinements

- **lactarius-torminosus.png** (enlarged follow-up): Recognizable salmon-pink zoned milkcap, but the margin looks like a thick scalloped cotton trim rather than shaggy fibres on an inrolled cap edge. Replace rounded lobes with irregular fine tufts; draw thinner closer cream gills. Keep pink zoning and white latex. This is a fidelity refinement, not a claim that the species is wholly wrong. [Aranzadi description, printed page 518](https://www.aranzadi.eus/assets/files/urola-kosta-bailarako-perretxikoak-espanol.pdf).

- **agaricus-campestris.png**: Pink/brown gills and lack of a volva are useful. Ring looks like a substantial fixed collar; make it more delicate if refining this icon. Visual/editorial suggestion; not independently source-validated in this audit.
- **collybia-rivulosa.png**: Underside is too schematic to clearly separate its attachment from Marasmius. Clarify slightly descending blades and cap surface zoning. Sources vary on gill spacing; do not make spacing alone a categorical error. [Reference](https://micologica-barakaldo.org/clitocybe-rivulosa/).
- **coprinus-comatus.png**: Deliquescent black edge and cylindrical cap are clear. White cap decoration reads as scalloped tiers; a few irregular shaggy flakes would convey the species more clearly. Visual/editorial suggestion; not independently source-validated in this audit.
- **hydnum-repandum.png**: Teeth are visible, but arranged like a fringe of long drops mainly at the rim. Distribute shorter teeth across the underside while retaining the irregular cream cap. Visual/editorial suggestion; not independently source-validated in this audit.
- **macrolepiota-procera.png**: Tall parasol silhouette and snakeskin stem are useful. The ring is simplified to a small single collar; a coherent double-edged ring would strengthen it without adding a second separate ring. [Reference](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/macrolepiota_procera.html).
- **morchella-esculenta.png**: Cap reads as isolated dark holes in a solid surface. Connected irregular honeycomb ridges would communicate the intended structure better; broad silhouette remains recognizable. Visual/editorial suggestion; not independently source-validated in this audit.
- **ramaria-aurea.png**: Colour and coral concept read correctly, but rounded finger-like branches are very sparse. A denser repeatedly branched silhouette would be more representative. [Reference](https://www.aranzadi.eus/buscador-micologico/ficha/1-2-005.24.03.00.64.00?from=gallery).
- **ramaria-formosa.png**: Pale base, salmon branches and yellow tips are useful. Add finer repeated branching; current outline looks like a few blunt fingers. [Reference](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/ramaria_formosa.html).

## What to retain

The broad colour and silhouette distinction between Amanita muscaria, pantherina, caesarea and the white/olive amanitas is useful; saclike volvas are visible on the latter icons. Boletes mostly retain cap colour, stout form and the intended netting distinction. The Tricholoma pardinum icon is appreciably stockier and more scaled than terreum. Craterellus cornucopioides reads as a hollow dark trumpet. These observations do not establish species identity from an icon.

The shared icon style can stay simple. Preserve the distinguishing kind of structure: true gill blades versus blunt folds, teeth versus a smooth underside, cracked cap cuticle versus colour patches, and a veil remnant versus a substantial skirt. Do not add unsupported photographic detail just for realism. Fine pore dots can legitimately disappear at small sizes; their omission alone was not classified as a wrong species.

The source comparisons above are limited to the stated morphological features, not edibility advice or proof of Catalan distribution. North American descriptions are used only for broadly shared structures; European mycological sources were preferred where available. Sources differ over the spacing of Collybia/Clitocybe rivulosa gills, so that icon remains a refinement rather than a categorical mismatch.
