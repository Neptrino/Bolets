# Field-card illustration review — 20 September 2026

Dated visual audit of the 62 active WebP field cards. Findings describe this exact artwork snapshot, not future revisions. No artwork was changed or published in this review.

## Scope and limits

All 62 cards were screened in eight contact sheets against their labels and versioned catalogue morphology. Twelve suspect or previously corrected cards received enlarged inspection; the companion JSON records that distinction and SHA-256 hashes. This is an editorial visual screening, not an independent mycological review or certification that unflagged species are accurate. Seasonal values, edibility wording, altitude ranges and all habitat illustrations were not independently fact-checked. No species identification or consumption decision should rely on these images.

Local inspection sheets are in `artifacts/field-card-review-2026-09-20/`. These are disposable local evidence; the report and checksum inventory remain versioned. Earlier PNG revisions were not the objects of this review.

## Priorities

- **P1 — correct first:** visible structural or explanatory problems. Fredolic gros, Apagallums, Pet de llop gros and Mataparent.
- **P2 — improve depiction:** exaggerated texture, poor demonstration of an important feature, or unrepresentative proportions. These are not all proven biological impossibilities.
- **Check — unresolved:** a specific concern needing a better reference comparison before deciding whether to redraw.
- **Screened:** no conspicuous mismatch found at the recorded inspection scale. This does not mean approved or exhaustively verified.

## Main findings and reference basis

1. **Fredolic gros (*Tricholoma portentosum*):** the image labels notched gills but renders rounded, descending folds. This is visible in the tilted specimen and panel 3. The immediate evidence is the contradiction with the card and catalogue morphology. [California Fungi species reference](https://www.mykoweb.com/CAF/species/Tricholoma_portentosum.html) provides a further reference for the species; its full page could not be retrieved in the final fetch, so this audit does not claim a complete textual validation from that page.
2. **Apagallums:** the image can teach “two separate rings.” Render a single coherent ring with a double edge. [Michael Kuo's description](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/macrolepiota_procera.html) describes the double-edged movable ring. That source explicitly discusses a North American species complex; use it for the broad ring concept, not as a complete validation of the European species.
3. **Pet de llop gros:** the sharp left-white/right-brown split needs separate age labels or separate specimens. This is an explanatory composition problem, not a claim that maturation is perfectly uniform.
4. **Mataparent:** the close-up turns pores into a labyrinth. [Kuo's collection-based description](https://mushroomexpert.com/tylopilus_felleus.html) describes circular pores and allows cap cracking; it also explicitly cautions about North American versus European taxonomy. The recommendation is to restore recognizable pore anatomy, not impose North American ecology or forbid all cap cracks.
5. **Cep and related boletes:** repeated raised netting and sculpted caps blur species differences. [Kuo's Boletus edulis description](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/boletus_edulis.html) supports fine reticulation as the comparison point. Extent can vary; do not arbitrarily erase every lower-stem reticulation.
6. **Amanitas and Cortinarius:** improve teaching clarity of sac edges and delicate veil remnants. The volva is present in the enlarged death-cap card; calling it absent would overstate the finding. Mature specimens can lose veil remnants. [Kuo's Amanita overview](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/amanita.html) and [Cortinarius overview](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/cortinarius.html) are genus-level context, not species-by-species sign-off.

The current Hydnum and Russula virescens corrections look improved. Lepiota brunneoincarnata is more clearly separated from the tall parasol composition. Tricholoma pardinum's corrected white stem is improved, but raised scales and the unclear notch still warrant refinement.

## Presentation

A 360-pixel-wide rendering of Fredolic gros was inspected locally (`phone-360.png`). The common name remains clear, while explanatory labels and the footer require enlargement. This applies to the dense shared template: a future Instagram adaptation should preserve access to the complete card while providing readable details separately. This audit did not render or approve an Instagram campaign.

Across the set, reduce generic glossy relief, distinguish thin gills from blunt folds and pores, and keep cut specimens consistent with intact examples. Static pictures do not demonstrate mobility, flexibility, smell or the timing of colour changes; those need explicit explanatory framing. Preserve the official logo and existing common-name hierarchy.
