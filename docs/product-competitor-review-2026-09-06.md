# Product competitor review — 6 September 2026

## Assessment

Boletada, Trobarbolets and Va de Bolets all compete for the decision of where and when to go mushroom hunting. A conditions map, species guide or calendar is not exclusive to Bolets. The current evidence does not establish that competitors have overtaken Bolets in feature breadth, prediction accuracy, adoption or release speed.

The practical priorities are a clear first visit, repeat use and evidence that the readings help. More catalogue entries alone will not address those priorities.

## Baseline and scope

Compared public pages with the [27 August SEO review](seo-competitor-comparison-2026-08-27.md) and [4 September Instagram review](instagram-competitor-comparison-and-growth-plan-2026-09-04.md). Checked Boletada's homepage, catalogue, calendar, cep and apagallums profiles and browser login gate; Trobarbolets' homepage, methodology and demo landing; Va de Bolets' weekly summary; bolets.com and Fotos de Bolets homepages; and Bolets' homepage and live current-conditions page.

Features absent from an earlier report are newly observed, not necessarily newly released. No accounts were created, subscriptions purchased, messages sent or tracking configuration changed. No new traffic, revenue, social-growth or comparative accuracy measurements were obtained.

## Boletada: direct subscription competitor

Its offer remains an annual predictor subscription: €19.99 for the first 100 subscribers, with €29.99 advertised as the usual annual price. Native apps are still described as forthcoming. The homepage concentrates on selecting a species and comparing favourable areas. [Homepage](https://boletada.cat/).

The public catalogue still reports **23 entries**, matching the 4 September baseline. It exposes search and month/edibility/rating filters. A separate annual table covers the edible entries. [Catalogue](https://boletada.cat/bolets/), [calendar](https://boletada.cat/temporada-de-bolets/).

The sampled cep and apagallums profiles include habitat, altitude, season, confusion advice and source links. This supersedes the earlier report's blanket statement that the guide had no sources. The check establishes source-link presence, not independent review or verification of every claim. [Cep](https://boletada.cat/bolets/cep/), [apagallums](https://boletada.cat/bolets/apagallums/).

The browser now shows a login gate at `/app/`. The earlier report's nine-species selector and detailed/station toggles could not be reverified. Do not describe those as a current tested inventory or infer paid features from the landing page. [Map entry](https://boletada.cat/app/).

**Interpretation:** credible competition for the same planning task. Its concentrated proposition is worth learning from, but there is no measured conversion advantage. Catalogue expansion is not demonstrated by these two snapshots.

## Trobarbolets: modelling claims and participation

Its homepage advertises daily municipal coverage and grants current-map access after review of a forest-cleanup video. The public demo is explicitly dated 25 September 2025. It also links comarca and species pages built around public observations. These are currently visible offerings, not dated release announcements. [Homepage](https://www.trobarbolets.cat/), [demo](https://www.trobarbolets.cat/demo).

The methodology describes explicit rules combined with gradient boosting trained on GBIF/iNaturalist observations. It reports 29 variables, 240 weather stations, cross-validation AUC of 0.956 and approximately 0.81 on later years. These are **operator-reported results**, not independently reproduced measurements; AUC does not establish calibrated probabilities or better field performance than Bolets. It also describes device-local saved places. [Methodology](https://www.trobarbolets.cat/info).

**Interpretation:** stronger public validation claims merit attention. Bolets should develop its own reproducible retrospective evaluation, with sampling bias and uncertainty explained. Preserve the separation between presence-only community data and live scoring; do not imitate another model's data policy merely for competitive parity.

## Va de Bolets: editorial planning and email

The weekly page now carries a **3 September 2026** data-reference date and practical commentary on recent rain and the coming weekend. It retains a zone table, a simple rain/temperature method and an invitation to receive a weekly email. Email delivery was not tested. [Weekly conditions](https://www.vadebolets.cat/mapa-boletaire-setmanal/).

**Interpretation:** useful competition on explanation and reasons to return, even without matching Bolets' ecological detail. The fresh dated briefing demonstrates maintained content, not a new feature release.

## Bolets today and recommended response

The live homepage advertises **62 profiles**, comparisons, a game, a field journal and generalized public findings. The current-conditions page loaded successfully in Chrome with 6 September readings, ranked species/territories, favourable-area coverage, limiting factors and map links. It also exposed timeline controls; animation playback was not tested. These are existing capabilities, not proposals. [Homepage](https://bolets.app/), [current conditions](https://bolets.app/bolets-avui).

1. **Improve the route to the existing answer.** Test whether a new mobile visitor can find a suitable territory/species and understand the reading within 30 seconds. Use the existing Avui experience; do not create another disconnected dashboard.
2. **Evaluate an opt-in return mechanism.** A digest or meaningful-change alert based on El meu bosc's saved territories/species is a candidate, subject to user demand. Measure return visits and useful map opens rather than notification volume. Existing Instagram publication already provides a return channel.
3. **Publish bounded validation evidence when available.** Show what was evaluated, its limitations and failures. More model inputs or smaller rendered cells do not prove greater accuracy.
4. **Keep original field material growing.** Improve useful, attributed photographic evidence and journal participation rather than targeting competitor catalogue counts.

These are prioritization recommendations, not an authorized implementation or monitoring schedule.
