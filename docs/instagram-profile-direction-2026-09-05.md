# Instagram profile direction · 5 September 2026

Reviewed the live @bolets.app profile and its first twelve covers. At review,
three pinned Reels used almost identical dark-green covers with small headlines
near the top and large blank areas. Older education/species templates use a
lighter typeface and gradients; newer campaign templates use a heavy display
face. The grid therefore mixes templates while repeating the same dark field.
Many headlines introduce application features. Mushroom photographs and the
actual maps are comparatively scarce or secondary.

## Direction

Keep the forest/cream/orange palette, but give most of the cover to a visible
subject. Use a short, readable headline and let details live inside the carousel
or caption. Prioritize public-facing questions and field observations over
repeated product introductions. This is a design judgment, not a claim about
measured reach or Instagram's ranking system.

The user prefers the actual Avui map as the weekend lead. The weekly Reel now
opens with the combined map and “Aquest cap de setmana · On miraries?”. It has
five frames: map, territorial comparisons, species detail, territorial extent,
and a save/share invitation. Keep the date, interpretation limits, and explicit
maximum/extent distinction. Do not label current conditions as a future forecast.

For the profile, replace the three similar pinned covers with visually distinct
entry points: the map, a species/identification guide, and a short introduction.
A proposed nine-cover direction uses six photographic covers, two map covers,
and one simple editorial question. That is a visual experiment, not a rigid
publishing quota. Use original field photos/footage where available and retain
reference-image attribution; never imply a catalogue photo is today's finding.

Simplify the profile bio and correct “Guía” to “Guia”. Suggested copy:

> Bolets de Catalunya, amb criteri. 🍄
> Mapa de condicions · Espècies · Guies de camp
> Prepara la sortida. Aprèn a mirar el bosc.
> ↓ Consulta el mapa d’avui
