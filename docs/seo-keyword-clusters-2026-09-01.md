# SEO keyword clusters and competitive gap analysis — 1 September 2026

## Executive verdict

Bolets Atles already has the right information architecture for the most valuable Catalan search journeys. The main problem is not a lack of pages: it is that a very new domain has not yet converted a strong catalogue into rankings.

The best near-term opportunity is the intersection of **current conditions, species and territory**. The strongest low-difficulty terms in the Spain database are `mapa bolets catalunya` (170 monthly searches, difficulty 5), `on trobar bolets aquesta setmana` (140, difficulty 5), `bolets catalunya` (390, difficulty 6), `bolets comestibles catalunya` (390, difficulty 9) and `on trobar bolets ara` (90, difficulty 7). Existing pages already match all five intents.

The most important missing content is narrower:

1. A small set of demand-backed species or terminology gaps: **sabatera**, **lleterola vermella**, **moixernó de tardor**, and a broad, safety-led **peus de rata** explainer.
2. Preserve and monitor the crawler-visible, dated content on `/bolets-avui`. A follow-up raw-response audit on 1 September confirmed that the complete normal and Googlebot HTML responses contain the territorial answer, update time and ranking as well as the streaming loading shell. No rendering rebuild is justified at present.
3. Better alias coverage on existing species pages, especially `pota de perdiu`, `fredolic tòxic`, `apagallums tòxic` and plural/common-name variants.
4. A selective post-harvest cluster—cleaning, storage and a few recipes—only after the current-conditions and species pages begin earning impressions. Recipes drive substantial traffic for bolets.com, but they are not the product's defensible advantage.

Do not respond by publishing dozens of near-duplicate location, singular/plural or Spanish pages. Query ownership should remain explicit and one intent should have one canonical destination.

## Research scope and limitations

- SE Ranking Spain database (`es`), retrieved 31 August–1 September 2026. The database contains Catalan and Spanish queries; `ca` would mean Canada, not Catalan.
- Keyword volume is an estimated average, not a traffic forecast. Similar spellings and singular/plural variants overlap and must not be added together as unique demand.
- Difficulty is SE Ranking's 0–100 vendor metric. It is useful for relative prioritization, not a promise of rankability.
- Controlled live SERP sample: Google desktop, Spain, Catalan language, 12 representative queries.
- Competitor traffic is SE Ranking's estimated organic traffic, rounded from the domain-page data. Small numbers are especially directional and seasonal.
- `bolets.app` has no organic domain rows in SE Ranking yet. Public search already returns the catalogue and current-conditions pages, so this is a maturity/data-lag baseline rather than proof of deindexation.
- Existing repository research remains authoritative for technical audit history, Search Console operations and editorial safety. This document is the quantitative keyword/competitor addendum that was previously blocked by connector reauthentication.

## Keyword clusters and canonical ownership

### Cluster 1 — Broad Catalonia discovery

**Queries:** `bolets`, `bolets catalunya`, `bolets de catalunya`, `bolets catalans`, `bolets a catalunya`.

**Owner:** `/`.

**Role:** Establish the category, explain the atlas and route users to the three principal jobs: know the species, understand the season and inspect current conditions.

**Action:** Keep “Bolets de Catalunya” in the title/H1 relationship, add a concise indexable paragraph that names the catalogue, current conditions, map and territorial guides, and link with descriptive anchors. Do not try to make `/bolets` compete for the same broad phrase.

### Cluster 2 — Current conditions and where to go now

**Queries:** `bolets avui`, `on trobar bolets avui`, `on trobar bolets ara`, `on trobar bolets aquesta setmana`, `on hi ha bolets ara a catalunya`, `donde hay setas ahora en cataluña`.

**Owner:** `/bolets-avui` for current territorial summaries; `/map` for map-specific variants.

**SERP expectation:** A dated answer, an immediately readable area summary and a path to the map. Va de Bolets currently ranks first or second for the core weekly query.

**Action:** Server-render a useful last-published territorial snapshot in the initial HTML. The inspected crawler view contains only “Preparant la lectura d'avui…”. This is a rendering risk, not proof that Google cannot execute the page, but it leaves the static answer weaker than the competitor's 32-zone table. Include a visible update time, data scope, best current territories, uncertainty and a link preserving the selected species/window.

### Cluster 3 — Map and prediction

**Queries:** `mapa bolets catalunya`, `mapa bolets avui`, `mapa de predicció de bolets`, `predicció bolets`, `mapa setas cataluña`.

**Owner:** `/map`.

**Action:** Keep the interactive map, but add a lightweight indexable explanation above or immediately below it: what the colours mean, what is current, how species selection changes the result and why it does not reveal exact locations. Link from `/bolets-avui`, all model-supported species pages and the two species-group hubs.

### Cluster 4 — Catalogue, names and identification education

**Queries:** `tipus de bolets`, `guia de bolets`, `noms de bolets`, `tipus de bolets comestibles`, scientific names and common-name aliases.

**Owner:** `/bolets` plus individual `/bolets/{slug}` profiles.

**Action:** Use the catalogue as the only generic guide hub. Add a short section explaining common names, scientific names, regional aliases and why a photo alone is insufficient. Keep search/filter controls, but ensure the most important explanatory content is present without interaction.

### Cluster 5 — Edibility and poisonous lookalikes

**Queries:** `bolets comestibles`, `bolets comestibles catalunya`, `bolets verinosos`, `bolets tòxics`, `bolets no comestibles`, `fredolic tòxic`, `apagallums tòxic`, `ceps verinosos`.

**Owners:** `/bolets-comestibles`, `/bolets-verinosos`, exact species profiles and `/compare/{slug}` pages.

**Action:** Keep the generic edible and poisonous clusters separate but cross-linked. Use complete safety language and exact lookalike comparisons. Do not target broad poisonous queries with a single species page. The highest-priority comparison refresh is fredolic versus fredolic metzinós because the exact modifier has 260 estimated searches at difficulty 8.

### Cluster 6 — Species and species groups

**Queries:** common names, scientific names, aliases, habitat, season, confusion and “on trobar” modifiers.

**Owners:** one profile per species; `/zones/ceps` and `/zones/rovellons` for plural group intent.

**Priority order:** camagroc, fredolic, carlet, apagallums, rovelló/pinetell, cep group, bolet de tinta, pota/cama de perdiu, rossinyol, llengua de bou, ou de reig.

**Action:** Put aliases in the introduction, metadata description, structured data and catalogue search index—not in duplicate URLs. Add new descriptive profiles only for distinct sourced species. Keep them outside maps, condition caches and quantified calendars until justified numerical ecology exists.

### Cluster 7 — Season and post-rain timing

**Queries:** `temporada de bolets`, `bolets de primavera`, `bolets d'estiu`, `bolets de tardor`, `bolets d'hivern`, month queries, `quan surten els bolets després de ploure`.

**Owners:** `/temporada`, `/temporada/{mes}`, the four season guides and `/quan-surten-els-bolets-despres-de-ploure`.

**Action:** Preserve this architecture. The editorial copy should explain that month is a baseline and current weather/altitude shifts fruiting. During September–November, link every seasonal page to `/bolets-avui`; outside peak season, surface the relevant spring/winter species instead of manufacturing autumn freshness.

### Cluster 8 — Territorial discovery

**Queries:** `bolets montseny`, `bolets berguedà`, `bolets cerdanya`, `bolets garrotxa`, `bolets ripollès`, `bolets prades`, species + place combinations.

**Owners:** `/zones/{area}`, `/zones/{area}/{species}` and the selected high-value local guides.

**Action:** Existing coverage is already substantial. Improve distinct local evidence, internal links and current summaries before adding areas. Candidate future areas should come from Search Console demand and verified coverage—not a Cartesian expansion. The most defensible gaps in the sampled SERPs are Cadí-Moixeró/Alt Urgell and Pallars Sobirà, but each needs a distinct sourced page and verified complete data.

### Cluster 9 — Post-harvest and recipes

**Queries:** `congelar bolets`, `com conservar els ceps`, `rovellons a la brasa`, `estofat de vedella amb bolets`, `pasta amb camagrocs`, `llengua de bou receptes`.

**Owners:** `/conservar-bolets` today; a future `/receptes` hub only if the product deliberately expands.

**Action:** First strengthen `/conservar-bolets` around cleaning, refrigeration, freezing, drying and species-specific caveats. Recipes are a real traffic pool—bolets.com's top recipe ranks first for `estofat de vedella amb bolets` (320, difficulty 5)—but recipe publishing should be a bounded editorial strategy, not filler. If approved, start with six demand-backed recipes and link each from the relevant species profile.

### Cluster 10 — Spanish demand

**Queries:** `donde hay setas ahora en cataluña` (320, difficulty 7), `setas catalunya` (260, difficulty 7), `setas comestibles en cataluña` (140, difficulty 7), `mapa de setas en cataluña` (70, difficulty 11).

**Owner:** None until there is a complete Spanish-language experience.

**Action:** Defer. A coherent `/es/` catalogue/current/map experience with correct language annotations could materially expand demand, but isolated translated articles would fragment trust and maintenance. Reassess after Catalan pages have 60–90 days of Search Console data.

### Live SERP observations

In the controlled 12-query desktop/Catalan/Spain sample, Bolets Atles did not appear in the top 10. Representative winners were:

- `bolets catalunya`: tourism/editorial pages, bolets.com and the institutional ACSA page.
- `bolets avui`: Va de Bolets first, followed by app roundups, bolets.com and current regional pages.
- `mapa bolets catalunya`: app roundups first; Va de Bolets, bolets.com, Trobarbolets, iFong and Boletòmetre all appeared.
- `on trobar bolets aquesta setmana`: Va de Bolets first; local news, destination articles and Bolets Berguedà followed.
- `temporada bolets catalunya`: news freshness, a commercial seasonal calendar, Bolets Berguedà and Boletaires.cat.
- `bolets comestibles catalunya`: catalogue/editorial pages, Ametller Origen and bolets.info.
- `guia de bolets`: Dos a la Tres, bolets.info, ACSA, books and 3Cat.

This shows that there is no single dominant competitor. Search results mix specialist catalogues, live-condition products, institutions, publishers, tourism pages, social/video and ecommerce. Bolets Atles should not try to beat all of them with one page; it should own the atlas/current/territory combination and use profiles as supporting topical authority.

### Where Bolets Atles is stronger

- A single versioned ecological source of truth connects species, habitat and predictions.
- More structured species-to-territory coverage than the sampled competitors.
- Explicit uncertainty and protection of sensitive locations.
- Strong comparison architecture for safety-sensitive lookalikes.
- Current conditions can be species-specific rather than a generic mushroom weather score.

### Where competitors are stronger

- Domain age, existing rankings and referring-domain history.
- Original field stories, videos and photography on bolets.com and specialist photo sites.
- A simple, immediately readable weekly table on Va de Bolets.
- Catalogue breadth: 69 advertised species on Boletaires.cat and bolets.info versus 53 canonical profiles here.
- Bilingual reach on Boletaires.cat and Spanish destination publishers.
- Recipes and post-harvest utility on bolets.com.

## Internal-linking model

Use a hub-and-spoke structure with descriptive anchors:

1. Homepage → current conditions, map, catalogue, season and zones.
2. Current conditions → relevant territory, winning species and exact map state.
3. Map → selected species profile, method and current conditions.
4. Species profile → group hub, exact comparisons, season/month and eligible territorial guides.
5. Territory → current reading, relevant species and local guide; never exact sensitive locations.
6. Safety/comparison → both species profiles and the generic poisonous hub.
7. Preservation/recipe → species profile; species profile may link back only when the advice is genuinely species-specific.

Avoid sitewide exact-match footer links to every long-tail page. Contextual links from the relevant hub provide a clearer topical graph.

## 30/60/90-day roadmap

### Days 0–30 — win existing-page intent

1. Add a response-level regression check that consumes the complete `/bolets-avui` HTML with a crawler user agent and requires the current-answer heading plus a truthful available, zero or unavailable state. The live response already passes this check; the purpose is to prevent regression.
2. Keep the existing title/H1/intro ownership for `/`, `/map`, `/bolets`, `/bolets-comestibles` and `/bolets-verinosos`. The source audit confirmed that the intended exact phrases and explanatory copy are already present.
3. Keep the existing `pota de perdiu`, toxic-fredolic and toxic-apagallums coverage. The source audit confirmed the alias, FAQs and comparison path are already implemented.
4. Publish the broad, safety-led peus-de-rata guide after its evidence and nomenclature gate; it is the largest distinct unowned Catalan cluster in the research set.
5. Use native Search Console to inspect the homepage, current page, map, catalogue and five priority species. Do not request indexing for the whole sitemap.
6. Acquire or commission original field images for camagroc, fredolic, rovelló, cep and rossinyol with provenance and non-sensitive location handling.

### Days 31–60 — fill evidence-backed gaps

1. Publish sabatera, lleterola vermella and moixernó de tardor as sourced descriptive profiles if the evidence package is ready.
2. Publish the broad peus-de-rata explainer only after the safety framing and nomenclature are reviewed against primary or institutional sources.
3. Strengthen cleaning/storage content and test whether it should remain one canonical preservation guide.
4. Improve the highest-impression territory pages rather than adding many new combinations.
5. Build a small digital-PR asset from non-sensitive aggregate data: for example, how the 2026 season moved across altitude bands, with method and uncertainty.

### Days 61–90 — expand only from observed demand

1. Export Search Console query × page data and find positions 8–20 with at least 10 impressions in a comparable 28-day window.
2. Resolve cannibalization where Google alternates between `/`, `/bolets`, `/map` and `/bolets-avui` for the same query.
3. Decide whether a tested recipe cluster is on-brand based on the performance of preservation and species pages.
4. Decide whether a complete Spanish layer is supportable; do not infer this solely from national search volume.
5. Add at most two new territorial entities, each backed by distinct demand, complete local data and official editorial sources.
