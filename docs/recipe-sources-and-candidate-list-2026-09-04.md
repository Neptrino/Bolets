# Recipe channel — freely usable sources and candidate recipes — 4 September 2026

Companion to `recipe-cms-and-content-plan-2026-09-03.md` (architecture, editorial and safety rules) and to the three approved recipe rows in `seo-keyword-map-2026-09-01.csv`. This document answers two questions: which recipe resources the site can use without buying or infringing anything, and which recipes are worth building as a search-discovery channel.

## Verdict

- Recipes are a real, winnable channel at the dish level. About 45 Catalan dish queries carry 20–590 estimated monthly searches at SE Ranking difficulty 4–8, and a zero-trust site (bolets.com) already holds positions 1–3 for the largest of them. The generic hub query `receptes de bolets` (20/month) is owned by media and supermarket blogs and is not worth targeting.
- bolets.com's recipes are not a moat. Recipe pages bring it 264 of its 6,326 estimated monthly visits (4%); the rain-data page brings 5,318. Recipes are still its second asset (26% of non-rain traffic) and its recipe pages win mostly by ranking for species names (llanegues, apagallums, llengua de bou, camagrocs), which our species profiles already target.
- Freely usable: Generalitat content under the Catalan open-information licence (ACSA, Canal Salut, Agricultura), Wikimedia text and images (CC BY-SA), public-domain cookbooks (Sent Soví, Llibre del Coch, La cuynera catalana, Agulló 1928), and CC-licensed photographs from Wikimedia Commons and Openverse. Not usable without permission: Gastroteca.cat (Prodeca, all rights reserved), the Corpus del Patrimoni Culinari Català, and every media, blog or competitor recipe. Dish names, ingredient lists and basic methods are not protected anywhere; prose, photographs and compilations are.
- Recommended launch collection: 12 pages (Tier 1). Keep the six approved demand-led and first-hand pages; promote fricandó, sopa de fredolics, canelons and conill from Batch 3 and add two new demand-backed pages (rodó de vedella amb bolets, crema de ceps). Demote risotto de ceps, truita de fredolics and arròs de galtes to Tier 2 on demand evidence.

## Method and limits

- Demand: SE Ranking Spain database (`es`), retrieved 4 September 2026, ~200 curated Catalan and Spanish queries plus related-keyword expansion of `receptes bolets`, `receptes rovellons` and `recetas setas`. Catalan long-tail volumes are under-reported in keyword databases; a null volume is not zero demand. Search Console remains the ground truth once pages exist.
- Search Console today shows no recipe query with impressions except `burger bolets` (18 impressions) and `menjar bolets` (1). Recipes would be a genuinely new query footprint.
- Competitor footprint: SE Ranking organic keywords for bolets.com (1,000 rows, complete for every keyword with estimated traffic above zero).
- SERP sample: one live Google desktop query (`receptes de bolets`, Spain, Catalan interface) captured 4 September 2026; a second query timed out.
- Licence checks: legal notices and licence pages fetched on 4 September 2026 and cited below. Copyright status of historical books follows Spanish law (80 years post mortem auctoris for authors who died before 7 December 1987; anonymous works of the period are public domain). This is research, not legal advice.

## Freely usable resources

### Legal baseline

- United States Copyright Office FAQ: "A mere listing of ingredients is not protected under copyright law. However, where a recipe or formula is accompanied by substantial literary expression in the form of an explanation or directions, or when there is a collection of recipes as in a cookbook, there may be a basis for copyright protection."
- European Union: the Court of Justice held in *Levola Hengelo* (C‑310/17, 13 November 2018) that the taste of a food product is not a "work". Spanish law (Llei de Propietat Intel·lectual, article 10) protects original expression, not ideas, methods or facts.
- Practical rule, already in the CMS plan: a dish, its ingredient list and its basic method are free to use; the prose, the photographs, the step wording and the compilation are protected. Every page is written and tested afresh, and every image is original or correctly licensed.

- National Spanish head terms (`risotto de setas`, `revuelto de setas`, `arroz con setas`): difficulty 66–78 and no Catalan-territory angle.

## Sourcing and safety rules per page

These restate the CMS plan so that the candidate list can be executed without reopening it.

1. Verify the dish is traditional or well established from at least two reliable references (Corpus dish list, Wikipedia, Agulló, La cuynera catalana, institutional recipes) and record them as context sources.
2. Develop and test an independent version; write every line afresh; never paste a Wikibooks, Gastroteca, media or bolets.com step.
3. Photograph the tested dish. Until then, use a Commons or Openverse image whose licence allows commercial use and modification, with author, licence and source stored in the media record and credited on the page.
4. Reuse the ACSA safety lines (cook wild mushrooms; 75 °C at the centre of roasts; identification is never made from the atlas) and link the species profiles that the recipe actually uses.
5. Mark every raw or undercooked preparation and give a cooked alternative; the apagallums and ou de reig pages keep their species-specific confusion warnings.
6. Record any open-licence text or CC image in `docs/data-licenses.md`.
