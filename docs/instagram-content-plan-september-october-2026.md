## Creative and production requirements

- Lead Reels with the question and the relevant visible detail in the first moments. Use readable Catalan subtitles and natural voiceover if available. Test the proposed duration rather than claiming it is universally optimal.
- Use the [visual guide](instagram-style-guide.md). Illustrated educational slides must follow the [approved field-guide style](instagram-illustrated-carousel-style.md), including the full soil logo. Photo observation posts should retain their photographic character.
- Keep one learning point per slide and one action per post. Use a few relevant Catalan topic/species keywords rather than a fixed hashtag quota.
- Select owned/licensed originals from the asset library, retain credits and generation provenance. Write alt text and verify covers/crops before Buffer upload.
- Source-check identification and ecological claims from the shared catalogue and cited guides before writing final copy; do not claim independent mycological review. No precise sensitive locations, fabricated predictions or claims that reference photos show today's findings.
- Batch production: record four distinct voiceovers and screen demonstrations in one session, prepare two carousels and choose two original photo stories. Refresh any current readings close to publication. Final captions and media remain production work, not completed assets.
- Complement Tuesday lessons with a Story question and Sunday photos with a response prompt. Native interactive stickers are a manual publishing step if the scheduler does not support them. Do not schedule a new automation for this plan.

## Measurement and decisions

Record each post's ID, format, publication time, promotion status, reach, views, shares, saves and comments at 48 hours and seven days. Use non-follower reach and average watch time only where native reporting actually provides them; missing is not zero. Record available website actions without attaching private map or finding context. Do not equate aggregate follower growth with followers attributable to a specific Reel.

Review the existing queue on September 20 before production booking. Review week one on September 28, and the complete experiment on October 12 so the final October 4 post has seven days of exposure. These are proposed editorial checkpoints, not created reminders.

Compare equal-age posts of the same format and known promotion status. Use medians and retain raw counts: a few extra shares on a small account are a useful hypothesis, not statistical proof. Continue a topic when multiple comparable posts improve its primary measure; rework the opening if it gets views but no intended action. Do not increase frequency or spend on views alone. No ad changes are part of this plan.
