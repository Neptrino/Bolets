# SEO competitor comparison — 27 August 2026

## Verdict

Bolets Atles has a stronger technical foundation and a more detailed species-to-territory experience than several of the sampled specialist sites. That does **not** establish that it currently outranks them. Fresh comparative rankings, estimated organic traffic, referring domains and domain-strength metrics could not be retrieved because the SE Ranking connection requires reauthentication.

The most useful next work is to improve demonstrable expertise, photographic evidence and a few specific content gaps—not to increase page count indiscriminately. The map is not unique by itself: Va de Bolets already competes directly for current-condition searches.

### Correct the “bolet de soca” brief before building it

The earlier plan was too narrow in proposing a single species profile as the main answer. Official terminology uses *bolet de soca* broadly for mushrooms growing on wood or weakened trees. The park and museum sources also use the term for bracket fungi. A source may use the same common name more narrowly, but that does not make it the universal interpretation. The appropriate first page is a disambiguating guide, with any species-specific safety comparison beneath it. [DIEC entry through Optimot](https://aplicacions.llengua.gencat.cat/llc/AppJava/index.html?action=Principal&clickLink=detall&database=DIEC&idFont=2790&idHit=2790&input_cercar=%22fong%22&method=detall&numPagina=8&numeroResultat=72&tematica=%28Totes%29&tipusCerca=cerca.queSignifica&tipusFont=%3Ci%3EDiccionari+de+la+llengua+catalana%3C%2Fi%3E+de+l%27Institut+d%27Estudis+Catalans+%282a+edici%EF%BF%BD%29&titol=bolet+), [Cadí-Moixeró fungal diversity](https://parcsnaturals.gencat.cat/ca/xarxa-de-parcs/cadi/el-parc/patrimoni-natural-i-cultural/fongs-i-liquens/diversitat-de-fongs/index.html), [Museu de les Terres de l'Ebre](https://www.museuterresebre.cat/pagina.asp?i=ca&id=248).

For *fals rossinyol*, the Societat Catalana de Micologia's published nomenclature explicitly associates the name with Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca and lists Omphalotus olearius separately. This supports the entity distinction, not a present-day consumption recommendation. [SCM paper, page 35](https://www.micocat.org/UNCINULA09/rcmPdf/RCM09_1985/27-37_APORTACIONES_A_LA_FLORA_MICOLOGICA_DE_CATALUNYA_Boletals_a_Catalunya.pdf).

## What not to expand automatically

- **Spanish content:** competitors offer Spanish experiences, but this is a separate product/editorial commitment, not a missing Catalan keyword. If pursued, use genuinely translated, reviewed pages at distinct URLs and appropriate language annotations. [Google multilingual guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).
- **Recipes, shopping and prices:** competitors cover these, but species profiles already include some culinary context. A recipe publication or store would expand the product's purpose; do not chase their total keyword counts indiscriminately.
- **More boilerplate regional pages:** existing coverage is substantial. Improve distinctive local evidence and linking first.
- **A schema-count contest:** structured data is not proof of expertise, a ranking guarantee or evidence that one site is winning.
- **Exact picking locations or overconfident predictions:** do not imitate these to make the product more clickable.

Google's guidance supports original, helpful material, clear authorship and demonstrable expertise; it does not recommend a target word count or producing extra pages merely to appear fresh. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
