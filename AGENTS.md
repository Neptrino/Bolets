# Repository Guidance

## Engineering principles

- Position Bolets as a mushroom reference for Catalonia: species, names, identification education, seasons, habitats and responsible field use. Lead homepage acquisition with predictions: explain the immediate benefit, make the map the primary action and show its preview directly after the hero. Keep learning and catalogue discovery prominent beneath that introduction; reference pages must remain useful without map access, accounts or scored ecology.
- Preserve the agreed homepage SEO wording when adjusting its marketing layout: H1 “Bolets de Catalunya. Mapa, espècies i temporada.” and title “Bolets de Catalunya: mapa, espècies i temporada”. Express the immediate prediction benefit through the supporting copy and map action.
- Prefer existing standard components, utilities, and project patterns before creating custom abstractions.
- Follow best practices for accessibility, maintainability, performance, security, responsive behavior, and clear naming.
- Keep changes focused and avoid unnecessary dependencies or unrelated refactors.
- Treat 500 lines as a review threshold and 1,000 lines as the hard limit for maintained runtime source files. Prefer cohesive domain extractions over mechanical slicing; keep reviewed exceptions and their reasons in `scripts/check-source-size.mjs`. Declarative version-controlled catalogues, immutable migrations, focused tests and ordered CSS may exceed the review threshold, but only the explicit catalogue exceptions may exceed the hard limit.
- Preserve the single source of truth for species ecology and prediction-model configuration.
- Reserve mathematical symbols such as `H`, `F`, `O`, `P`, `W`, `T`, and `E` for the technical method and internal documentation. In normal product interfaces, use complete plain-language labels and explain the relationship without requiring users to decode a formula.
- Build standard light content pages with `PageShell`, `PageHeader`, and `SectionHeader`; extend their narrow layout/tone variants instead of adding page-specific title, gutter, or vertical-spacing rules. Keep full-bleed home, species, map, method, and territorial heroes explicit when their composition is genuinely different.
- Prefer accessible, horizontally scrollable and compact tables for comparable admin collections; reserve cards for dashboard priorities, summaries, or records whose actions cannot be scanned usefully as columns.
- Use solid background colours throughout the product interface; do not use CSS gradients for page, section, card, banner, or control backgrounds.
- Keep the MapLibre stylesheet imported from `app/globals.css`, not from the client map component: its control stack must be ready beneath the prediction canvas for map gestures and controls to remain usable.
- Copy MapLibre's worker and shared ESM files together from the installed package during development and image builds. Set the shared map factory's explicit versioned worker URL before constructing maps; Next's bundled automatic worker URL does not preserve this module pair. Serve these generated assets through Next with immutable browser caching and opportunistic offline caching.
- Calculate browser prediction rasters in the bundled raster worker using the same pure pixel function as signed server maps. Keep one active and one replaceable pending job per map, discard superseded results, terminate on effect cleanup, and fall back to the shared calculation if workers fail. Reuse the last raster only for an unchanged ordered cell snapshot and camera; translate/scale its existing pixels during north-up gestures while a new viewport is calculated. Keep species/day replacements and final timeline paints atomic.
- Load feature-only styles from their owning route or component, preserving the order of related foundation/component/responsive files. Keep shared styles and MapLibre CSS in the root. Use `IntentLink` for navigation and acquisition links that would otherwise prefetch heavy destinations on first paint; preserve hover, focus and touch intent. Keep optional heatmap scripts on idle loading and video media on user-requested playback. Load auth and resumable-upload clients for finding synchronization only when the local outbox needs them.
- Preserve the interactive map's centre and zoom across species changes through the shared map layout's in-memory viewport. Do not restart automatic geolocation on a restored view, and honour newly requested territory bounds. Never persist this viewport in browser storage or analytics.
- Do not ship native browser popup UI for product selectors or confirmations. Use shared accessible site components instead of bare `<select>` elements or `window.alert`, `window.confirm`, and `window.prompt`; preserve native form semantics underneath when the component library supports them.
- Set font sizes only through the type-scale tokens defined in `app/globals.css` (`--text-xs` … `--text-3xl` for fixed text, `--display-xs` … `--display-xl` for fluid titles); never hardcode px/rem sizes or new `clamp()` expressions. The only exceptions are the OG/share image generators and the few decorative watermark numerals already marked in the stylesheet.
- Document important architectural decisions and update this file when project conventions change.
- Keep active documentation indexed in `docs/README.md`. Move dated research and completed release receipts, with their evidence companions, to `docs/archive/`; label historical status and repair inbound links. Remove redundant handoffs once their useful instructions are retained in current guidance or an archived receipt.
- Keep the repository map in `README.md` aligned with folder ownership. Retain dated research outside session scratch directories; exclude local `social/`, `artifacts/`, `output/` and `.codex-tmp/` material from the Docker build context. Omit local creative exports and `video/assets/` from source/release archives through committed `.gitattributes` export rules, and check the archive against the VPS 256 MiB transport limit before upload.

## Testing and verification

- Write unit tests whenever practical, especially for schemas, scoring logic, data adapters, and reusable utilities.
- Add integration or end-to-end coverage for critical user flows and external-data boundaries when appropriate.
- After meaningful changes, run the relevant tests, type checks, linting, and build checks.
- Do not consider a change complete until failures are investigated or explicitly documented.

## Performance and optimization

- Perform periodic optimization reviews using measurements, profiling, or bundle analysis.
- Avoid premature optimization; prioritize user-visible bottlenecks and evidence-based improvements.
- Keep server-rendered content lightweight and isolate interactive client-side behavior to the components that need it.
- Proxy the apex and www website through Cloudflare with Full (strict) TLS. Cache only the public optimized-media, Next.js static and icon paths using origin Cache-Control; bypass all other website paths at the edge. Keep Grafana Caddy charts labelled as origin measurements because edge cache hits do not reach the VPS. The live rules and rollback are documented in `deploy/vps/cloudflare.md`.
- Keep service-worker cache writes off response delivery with `FetchEvent.waitUntil`; serve versioned optimized WebPs cache-first even for direct navigations, while preserving dated network-first predictions and private-route exclusions.
- Serve only exported build assets (`/media/optimized/`, `/_next/static/`, `/icons/`) directly through Caddy from the candidate release's read-only `.static` directory. Export from the built image before activation so rollback restores matching assets. Keep generated field-card files release-local and content-keyed; coalesce and serialize cold renders.
- Cache validated timeline environments in compressed form and final scored frames separately, for up to 24 hours with unchanged scoring inputs. Key both caches by the completed observed-condition and forecast publication identity; check publication metadata every five minutes and forecast age on every generation lookup, and retain a one-minute fallback when that lookup fails. Bound heavy spatial reads to two active jobs per process/worker with one background slot and finite waiting queues. Select the timeline date before loading frozen station windows and reuse immutable cell-temperature patches; reserve the 512 MiB Edge memory limit for the spatial reader. Warm today’s canonical public buckets and the eight 5 km timeline days with bounded concurrency and resumable progress after completed publication generations; use a separate host-generated warming credential, never the metrics or ingestion token. Buffer at most two upcoming animation frames through the shared bucket request gate, cancel speculative queues on navigation or visibility changes, and expire browser timeline buckets after one minute. Render the Avui map independently of the streamed territorial summary.
- Record public request timings only under fixed route-group labels, excluding private routes, private referrers and Do Not Track. Remove request objects and response headers before logging. Export process-only event-loop and memory metrics through independent authenticated internal ingress so database failures cannot hide runtime health.
- Serve version-controlled catalogue and editorial WebP imagery through the build-time responsive variants generated by `npm run media:build`; use `MediaImage` for `MediaAsset` rendering and `StaticMediaImage` for other local WebPs so they never enter Vercel's runtime image transformer. Keep the version segment in `src/lib/static-media.ts` in sync with any change to source imagery or encoding policy so immutable browser caches receive a new URL.

## Project-specific architecture

- Keep species knowledge profiles in version-controlled, validated data files.
- Keep scientific `speciesId` values as stable internal identifiers for ecology, predictions and stored data. Publish species detail pages through the explicit Catalan slugs in `data/species-slugs.ts`, require one unique slug for every catalogue species, and permanently redirect the former scientific-name URLs to their Catalan canonical URLs.
- Keep descriptive species that lack a justified numerical ecology in `data/reference-species.ts`, separate from scored `SpeciesProfile` records. Merge them through `data/catalogue.ts` for public discovery, profile routes and the sitemap, but never include them in map selectors, condition/habitat caches or quantified monthly calendars. Show sourced habitat and season text, not fabricated altitude, pH, thermal ranges or phenology scores. Share the standard species hero, gallery and cards across both kinds of profile.
- Publish source-backed identification guides as editorial information with explicit safety limits and truthful authorship. No independent mycological review is planned for now: state its absence without implying a pending review, and do not impose an expert-review indexing gate. Source checks and AI-assisted editing are not expert sign-off. If independent review is ever added, record the actual reviewer identity, scope, date and approval before claiming it; never add prediction ecology just to target a search term.
- Ensure the prediction engine consumes the same ecological configuration used by species pages.
- Clip ecological and prediction grids to the version-controlled ICGC Catalonia land boundary before rendering.
- Keep Supabase access server-side and protect database boundaries appropriately.
- Keep ad-hoc maintenance state in session-local temporary tables or a private schema. Any retained maintenance table in `public`, including restored scratch tables, must enable RLS and revoke all access from `public`, `anon`, and `authenticated` while preserving required server-role access.
- Deploy production self-hosting as a thin Bolets Compose override on a pinned official Supabase `self-hosted/v*` release. Keep the API gateway, database pooler and Studio loopback-only behind the shared TLS proxy; preserve the upstream Edge Function router when synchronizing repository functions; and rehearse every Supabase update against a verified off-host restore before production.
- Deploy every tested `main` commit through the forced-command GitHub Actions identity into an immutable VPS release directory. Build and smoke-test the Linux amd64 application image once in Actions in parallel with verification, publish it to GHCR, and gate deployment on both jobs succeeding. Deploy only its immutable digest alongside the matching source archive. Keep daily-overview readiness synchronous and schedule optional map warming through the background systemd service. Verify the image revision and compiled public configuration before migrations or activation; never rebuild the application on the VPS. Keep registry authentication in a temporary root-only Docker config using the job token, retain release image metadata and local images for rollback, and preserve the legacy source protocol only for transition and older releases. Keep production secrets and the pinned Supabase checkout outside application releases, serialize deployments, and retain automatic application rollback on health-check failure.
- Treat `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations` as the production database source of truth. The VPS rollout must discover every ordered file in `supabase/migrations`, apply each pending migration atomically before synchronizing functions, and fail closed on missing, remote-only or non-contiguous versions. Never edit an applied migration; express every subsequent database change in a newly generated migration file.
- Treat the Open-Meteo AWS Lambda as a narrow HMAC-authenticated egress adapter, not an independent scheduler or quota owner. Keep one leased AWS shard alongside the VPS and Cloudflare lanes, record aggregate provider usage transactionally in PostgreSQL without applying a local quota, cap Lambda reserved concurrency, and deploy immutable Lambda versions through a repository-and-production-environment-scoped GitHub OIDC role without stored AWS keys.
- Run product analytics through a digest-pinned self-hosted Umami service with its own PostgreSQL database and backup. Keep its direct port loopback-only, bootstrap the administrator and fixed website ID before publishing DNS, respect Do Not Track, and never collect URL query strings or fragments that may encode map state. Heatmaps may sample anonymous clicks and scroll depth only on public routes; keep session replay disabled and fail closed before recorder payloads leave the browser if a URL is private or cannot be sanitized. Performance measurement is limited to analytics-eligible public pages. Conversion and funnel events may record only allowlisted event names on a neutral virtual path: never attach account identifiers, species, finding details or the private route context.
- Keep manual ingestion controls behind the signed Supabase Auth session, a server-validated non-user-editable `app_metadata.app_role = admin` check and a same-origin server route. Dispatch through a service-role-only database function that reads the ingestion token from Vault; never expose operational credentials to the browser. Resume incomplete spatial generations, reset only completed selected generations, and leave the last complete observed condition caches published until their replacements finish.
- Keep the private administration area organized around `/admin` as an actionable overview, with first-class management routes for users, findings, reports, contributions and publishing. Keep operational telemetry and manual pipeline controls isolated at `/admin/operacions`, not mixed into the overview. All private pages share the protected route-group shell, verify the administrator role again beside every sensitive read and mutation, and remain excluded from analytics and indexing. The operations console reuses the signed Supabase application session and keeps the independently generated internal ingress token reserved for metrics. It reads one `security invoker` service-role-only status RPC and never returns ingestion metadata or secrets. Present atmosphere, soil, forecast and score-cache publication as distinct products; keep rolling provider memory explicitly labelled as internal continuity state. Its only mutation surface is the allowlisted same-origin resync dispatcher described above. Export only bounded aggregate pipeline metrics to the internal collector. Run optional Grafana Alloy from a digest-pinned image with no public listener, no Docker socket, read-only Docker log access, and only the host `/proc` and `/sys` mounts required for its allowlisted collectors.

Keep hourly-lapse-only recounts as rejected shadow comparisons, and re-evaluate any definition or station-pool change against stations, findings and boundaries before versioning it. Preserve validation flags, UTC interval starts and complete measurement intervals; published provisional readings are not final-validated observations. ## Environmental pipeline conventions

- Ingest regional and cell weather through authenticated Supabase Edge Functions; never call providers directly from the browser.
- Keep the observed AROME and seamless-precipitation streams as exact private 720-hour rolling provider states. Fetch a 72-hour UTC overlap on ordinary days and fail closed on gaps instead of shortening any scoring window. Lease stable 100-point database shards as sequential 50-location provider batches through exactly three independently leased egress lanes (VPS direct, one authenticated Cloudflare Worker, and one authenticated AWS Lambda), require every fallback shard before claiming atmospheric work, repair only linked fallback points if a provider-hour boundary makes their axes lag, and publish the generation cursor only after every shard succeeds. Every Open-Meteo consumer and lane must record conservatively estimated usage (including a 5% safety multiplier) through the same atomic minute, hour and day ledger, but the application must not enforce a local quota. A provider 429 must trip only that lane's database-backed circuit breaker, honour bounded `Retry-After` or exponential backoff, and release the shard for a healthy lane. Do not add relay zones, retry a blocked lane outside its circuit breaker or expose a general-purpose proxy. Sample the seamless fallback on the existing 500-point / 9 km `best_match` lattice and align it by exact hour to each 2.5 km AROME point before applying `station-rain-v1`; XEMA remains the primary rain source wherever at least two complete nearby gauges are available.
- Keep Copernicus CLMS surface-soil-moisture and soil-water-index products in a private, quality-aware shadow stream until a versioned calibration validates a single-source bridge into the unified water model. Preserve raw flags, product-specific SSF semantics, product times/version, completion state, 1 km native resolution and actual sample spacing; never relabel relative percentage as volumetric moisture, score SSM and SWI independently, or splice CLMS days into the production Open-Meteo moisture window. Treat the bounded relational hot window as operational preview data, not as sufficient seasonal calibration history.
- Import 250 m static terrain, land-cover, and soil evidence only through the service-role importer with source and verification metadata.
- Treat ICGC 1:50,000 geological units as display-only contextual evidence: store their mapped coverage and provenance separately from soil scoring inputs, never interpret map scale as metre resolution, and aggregate coarse geology area-weightedly from canonical 250 m cells.
- Calculate the hydrothermal fruiting-conditions index (`F`) and territorial opportunity index (`O`) on the Next.js server with the same resolved, versioned species model used by profile pages.
- Bind the 1, 2.5, 5 and 10 km materialized condition caches to the completed atmosphere/soil cursor generation, not only to `snapshot_date`. Same-day restores and replays must advance the corresponding `spatial-condition-*` marker after rebuilding so an earlier partial generation cannot be mistaken for the current cache. Retry publication through the database-local `refresh-spatial-condition-caches` job so the 1 km rebuild is not constrained by PostgREST's request timeout, and key long-lived Next.js overview caches by both published condition generations.
- Treat guild parameters and species overrides as explicit low-confidence priors in `data/model-priors.ts`. Derive smooth phenology anchors from the profile's single structured seasonality calendar, and initialize each supported species' thermal optimum and half-response widths from its versioned numeric climate envelope, with explicit species-literature overrides taking precedence; never derive numeric responses from editorial prose or renormalize around a missing component.
- Withhold the dynamic indices when any required static or hydrothermal input is unverified or stale. A verified zero for habitat, altitude or phenology may resolve opportunity to zero without substituting missing inputs.
- Treat each species' version-controlled habitat altitude range as its ecological core: score 100 through the interior, taper linearly to 75 during the 100 m inside either documented limit, and decline linearly to zero across the 100 m outer uncertainty margin. Cells at or beyond the margin's zero-score edge must not be painted as compatible.
- Preserve sampled land-cover fractions in every imported 250 m cell. Let `C` be the exact cover/pH-compatible fraction used by the habitat map and `A` the altitude taper within that compatible cover; effective habitat is `H = C × A`. Zero effective habitat and zero smooth phenology resolve opportunity to zero; never infer coarse compatibility from a union of labels or promote a partial match to 100%.
- Weight distribution-map blue intensity by the shared altitude edge taper, while retaining raw compatible coverage and altitude suitability as distinct inputs to `H`.
- For coarse predictions, derive altitude suitability inside compatible habitat as `sum(coverage × altitude taper) / sum(coverage)` from canonical 250 m cells; never score the arithmetic mean elevation of a mixed parent cell. Cell opportunity already contains `H`, so regional opportunity summaries must be equal-area and must not weight by habitat a second time. Paint positive prediction cells with their opportunity score-band colour; render verified zero neutrally with a discontinuous outline so colour means a positive response, while keeping withheld cells visually distinct.
- Build comarca, massís and paratge comparator readings from the fixed 1 km bucket lattice. Score every in-season edible species that has a published local guide from one shared environment payload, let the highest current 1 km cell choose the displayed species, and publish positive-cell and 20+-cell prevalence beside that best-cell value so one isolated square is never presented as representative of the whole territory. Calendar and editorial priority may order or gate candidates, but must not choose the winner. Territorial map links must preserve the species and exact scoring window.
- Model water once: normalize shallow-soil moisture by the verified texture class, combine it with distributed rain, ET₀, dry-spell memory and vapour-pressure deficit, and do not add rain, soil moisture or humidity again as independent factors.
- Preserve 3/7/14/21/26/30-day rain and ET₀, rainy-day counts, days 8–30 rain, dry-spell length, 7-day shallow-soil moisture memory, 7/14/20-day temperature means, and 14/20-day frost/heat exposure end to end.
- Past precipitation is the versioned `station-rain-v1` source: XEMA gauge inverse-distance hours where at least two complete stations sit inside the cutoff, the seamless Météo-France blend as the only model fallback, and AROME retained for thermal/humidity/wind fields. Keep half-hour completeness counts and bounded retention on the gauge stream, never fabricate gauge values for uncovered cells, record `precipitationSource` and gauge coverage on every snapshot, and re-validate with `weather:compare-station-rain` plus the private findings replay before changing the correction's definition or fallback model.
- Cache current-map station temperature adjustments independently of species, keyed by the versioned algorithm, immutable source references/weights, exact cell centre/elevation and all original thermal controls. Store only corrected thermal fields and provenance in the private bounded cache. Cache deterministic baseline decisions only with complete immutable inputs. Never cache missing optional evidence, substitute a full cached environment, or let a cache hit bypass freshness checks; failures fall back to the same pure calculation.
- Request map cells one bucket at a time. `bucketsForBounds` enumerates the fixed per-resolution lattice a viewport covers, and `src/lib/map-request-url.ts` is the only place a bucket request URL is spelled, so the live map and any pre-downloaded copy produce byte-identical URLs for the same ground. Never rebuild a request from raw viewport bounds: a viewport-shaped URL cannot be enumerated ahead of time and defeats both caching and offline replay. Merge bucket responses by `cellId`, share one abort signal and one in-flight registry per species/layer run so overlapping pans never fetch the same bucket twice, and report a viewport that resolved only in part as incomplete rather than as complete.
- Offer the smoothed prediction surface as a rendering of accessible cells on a fixed 2.5 km source grid; retain the access and timeline floors after that choice. While smoothing remains active, zoom must only magnify this field, never replace its scores, habitat fractions or zero mask with another grid. On interactive maps, automatically show exact cells when the access floor and viewport budget permit a grid finer than 2.5 km; restore smoothing automatically on zoom-out. Do not offer a representation selector or read a saved rendering preference. Public viewers remain smoothed, static previews retain their requested rendering, and the combined-map 1 km and timeline 5 km floors still apply. Exact sectors retain adaptive 250 m–10 km detail within access limits. Keep the production coverage and low-score opacity fades, include neighbours within the smoothing kernel through canonical bucket requests, and anchor raster sampling to geography. Full-Catalonia smooth views intentionally use the same 2.5 km source as the Avui preview; keep request concurrency and raster size bounded rather than switching to a coarse opacity field. Interpolate on the client; do not serve finer data through smoothing.
- Record provider state in `pipeline_sources` and every ingestion attempt in `ingestion_runs` so degraded and blocked sources remain visible.
- Colour 5 km and 10 km prediction cells by the habitat-coverage-weighted mean of the 2.5 km sectors inside them and detail them by the best sector, both derived from the same cached 2.5 km buckets the zoomed-in map reads; never max-pool the colour, a mean is what keeps the overview consistent with the smoothed zoomed-in surface; never score a blended coarse environment for the current map, and keep coarse identity and geometry on the cell while `summarisedFrom` names the sector whose reading and history it carries.
